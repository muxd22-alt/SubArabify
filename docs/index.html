Automatic Arabic Subtitles for Your Media

SubArabify monitors your movie folders and translates English subtitles to Arabic — completely on-device, offline, with zero data sent to the cloud.

100% Offline

ML Kit's on-device model runs entirely on your phone. No internet needed after initial model download.

⚡

Background Monitoring

WorkManager scans your media folders periodically and auto-translates new subtitle files.

🎯

Smart Detection

Automatically finds videos missing Arabic subtitles and creates .ar.srt files alongside them.

✏️

Thmanyah Sans Font

Generated subtitles use the premium Thmanyah Sans typeface for beautiful Arabic readability.

How It Works

1

Select Your Library

Use Android's Storage Access Framework to pick your Movies or TV Shows folder.

2

Enable Monitoring

Toggle background scanning. SubArabify checks for new media at your chosen interval.

3

Enjoy Arabic Subs

English .srt files are translated on-device. The Arabic .ar.srt file appears next to your video.
